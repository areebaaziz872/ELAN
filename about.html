ABOUTÉLAN

THE IDEA

ÉLAN is a concept for a contemporary label built on one idea: clothing is structure in motion.

It asks what a garment should do once the person wearing it starts to move, and designs backwards from the answer.

THE FORM

Silhouette comes first. Lines are drawn long and calm, with volume placed where the body needs room.

Wool gives structure, silk gives fall, cotton keeps things honest. Colour stays within ink, ivory, stone and a single deep burgundy.

THE PROCESS

Drawing, toile, fitting, recutting. The sequence is slow on purpose.

A garment is finished when it moves well in a room, not when it looks right on paper.

ÉLAN is a fictional brand created as a web design concept.
