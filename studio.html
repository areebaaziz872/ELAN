DESIGNINGINMOTION.

PHILOSOPHY

ÉLAN begins with a question about how cloth behaves when a person moves. Each piece starts as a drawing, becomes a toile, and is cut, pinned and recut until it holds its shape without forcing it. We prefer fewer decisions, made carefully: a seam should have a reason, a fabric should suit its cut, and a silhouette should still read when the wearer walks away.

  • Wool, silk and cotton, chosen for how they hang, hold and move.

  • Long lines and quiet volume, drawn before anything is cut.

  • Every garment is judged in motion, not on a hanger.
